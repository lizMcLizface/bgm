## Phase 5 additional expression — tonic avoidance, atonal sets and bitonality, 17 October 2026

The user requested a deliberate unsettling-harmony layer, then authorised atonal and dual-tonal modes and a dedicated category. Added six append-only progression choices (indices 25–30) under Unresolved · never the tonic. They use degrees II–V without a tonic chord, remain outside both normal and wild progression/length dice, and keep their own progression-drift pool. Section cadences, conductor pivots and requested endings cannot impose a tonic on these progressions. Tonic pitches can still occur inside other chords: this bank is unresolved tonal writing, not itself fully atonal.

Music now offers Shared tonal centre, Atonal · chromatic pitch sets, and Bitonal · two tonal centres. The harmonic-language choice is also represented in The Rules. The atonal mode uses a deterministic twelve-class row with seeded transposition, inversion and retrograde; successive small pitch sets replace diatonic harmony, and all twelve classes are available to melody. It has no functional tonic/dominant cadence or tonic pedal. It is a pitch-set generator, not a strict serial composition system or a reproduction of a score. Bitonality currently has two fixed role groups: backing (pad/bass) and foreground (lead/second lead), using the selected scale at centres separated by 1–11 semitones. Foreground transposition happens before note-expression/connection planning; rhythm and within-group intervals remain intact. Arbitrary independent modes per individual voice are a possible later extension, not implemented here.

Added explicit Flat 2nd / minor 9th clash and Chromatic major 7th chord colours. The first adds a chromatic semitone above the chord root in the upper octave; the second explicitly supplies the root's major seventh even on minor chords. Both are excluded from ordinary colour randomisation. They fit the existing reserved three-bit colour field; progressions fit the existing five-bit field. Tonality settings append a new music-code group, preserving old option indices and widths. Chord explanations identify actual pitch-set/dual-centre/unresolved decisions and explain why functional arrival rules are overridden. Scale controls show the chromatic field when atonal; two-centre mode offers a melody-key offset.

Added Altman as an original haunted string scene: atonal sets, flat-second friction, slow held pad/bass connections, continuous pitch motion and sparse independent melodic lines. The direction comes from the intimate string writing discussed in [Jason Graves' Dead Space 2 notes](https://www.jasongraves.com/dead-space-2); no melody or transcription was copied. Listening feedback approved the initial result but requested a string lead instead of Lament Lead. Altman now uses a new named Haunted Viola: two saw voices with a quiet triangle body, a 320 ms bowed onset, sustained body, long release, restrained air/vibrato and moderate room depth. Its sound identifier is appended to share codes. This is a synthetic viola-like patch, not a new recorded/physical bowed-string model.

New category Unsettling contains Altman, Seaside Hearth, Almost Home, Drone and Drift, Desert dusk, Damp Crater, Dark Mirror Dimension, Ice Cave Echoes and Haunted Staircase. Prior preset slugs remain valid. Category cards label atonal scenes correctly instead of implying an unused diatonic scale. The Rules replaces Explore the rulebook; its extra subtitle is removed. Its surface, rounded corners and transparent header now match other panels, including their fluid-background translucency. Browser checks verified the same background in both themes, with/without backdrop, and all nine category entries.
