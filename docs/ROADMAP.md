# Roadmap

Single list of open work, updated 2026-10-28. Finished plans live in `docs/archive/`. The detailed live plan for composition is [COMPOSITION_PLAN.md](COMPOSITION_PLAN.md).

## 1. Built, waiting for ears
Nothing here is known to be right until it has been heard in the app.

- **Melodic identity** (`settings.melodyPlan` noodle / same / change, `settings.melodyStyle`, `src/app/composition/melodicStyle.js`, Melody pills in the control panel, 2026-11-01): a preset is a style (song, lounge, hook, game, cinema, riff, or auto from its settings), a seed is a 4-bar song in it, written over the preset's chords; statements come back as written. Change turns the song into another over 10-15 minutes, a bar of the tune every 3 minutes or so. 98 presets default to Change with a written-out style (`tools/assignMelodyPlans.mjs`, `--off` undoes it); ambience, speech backgrounds, unsettling, demos and older versions stay on Noodle; none of the 98 has been heard individually. The bass and a rhythmic chord part join the song (`settings.melodyParts`, `composition/songParts.js`); the Melody row in the control panel picks plan, style and parts. Open: is Change good over a long listen, and do the assigned styles suit their presets (go through them in the presetter); programmed sequencer lanes keep their chord rhythm, and only their bass takes the tune's shape.
- **Game soundtrack presets** (2026-11-01, in Console Nostalgia): Crystal Prelude, Corridors of Slightly Late, Rain Over the Bug Capital, Summit Before Breakfast, Emerald Loop Zone, Two A.M. at the Tanuki Shop. Split voice with the pad an octave down, a song, flairs. Balance metered against Cold Drifts and Uplifting Trance, not yet heard.
- Composition controls (all in COMPOSITION_PLAN status table): `padRoot`, `chordSize`, `leadInertia`, `padContour`, `leadTendency`, `bassEuclid`, `borrowStyle`, `padVibrato`, generated progressions. Most have no presets seeded yet.
- Drum styles twostep, breaks, synthwave and their presets (Outrun After Midnight, Rainfall Breaks, Wet Pavement Two-Step). Check mobile CPU at 170 bpm.
- Step sequencer on the main page (`src/app/sequencer.js`, `SequencerPanel.jsx`, `settings.sequencer`, share part `x`.q): /synths beats and arrangements played exactly on the 808/909 with a stage per chord slot, plus Warehouse Lift, Acid Jack Afterhours, Electro Story, Dub Pulse Basement. Unheard, loudness unmeasured. Lanes (`sequencer.lanes`): chord and bass played as stabs on `pad` / `bass` rows with new tight voices (Supersaw Stab, Techno Stab, Pluck Chord, Rolling Bass; levels guessed) and a kick pump on pad and bass. Moves (`sequencer.moves`, per stage): phaser / chorus / flanger / saturation / delay / reverb / sends on the shared bus ramped across each chord, set per stage in the panel and in the four presets; fills (`sequencer.fills`) play the Fill stage every fourth bar; the panel picks the drum machine and its sound. Programming follows the conventions in `DANCE_PROGRAMMING.md` (swing, played hats, shaker, syncopated bass, fills every 8 bars, kick cuts, synced delay). Sections (`sequencer.sectionBars` 4 / 8 / 16): a stage lasts that many bars whatever the chords do, moves and kick cuts follow the section; sweepable mix filter (`filter.*` moves); portamento per voice; Drive Lead and Acid Lead voices (levels guessed). More presets on it: Boom Bap Bakery, Trap House Tuesday, Dusty Vinyl Sunday (hip hop), Uplifting Trance Departures, Minimal Click Lab, Slow House Sunset, Deep Pocket Daydream, Disco Ride Afternoon (minimal tech and slow chill house on the 808), Progressive Night Bus (trance; new Trance beats and a trance lane default). Unheard. Not done: a stage change on a section boundary rather than per chord, odd meters only tile the 16 steps, no playhead in the grid.
- **Iowa and VSCO instruments** ([IOWA_INSTRUMENTS.md](IOWA_INSTRUMENTS.md), 2026-11-02, uncommitted): 45 voices in the instrument list (`Iowa & VSCO`) and on `/synths/iowa`, 12 showcase presets in From Recordings, each tagged `fits:<category>` for later merging, none heard yet. Open: listen and merge; guitar's beating is not modelled; crotales' lowest keys stay about 9 dB under; go through the presets marked `[to check instrument]` in the presetter; decide what happens to the dead instruments in [INSTRUMENT_INVENTORY.md](INSTRUMENT_INVENTORY.md); the 21 demos are retired as hidden presets, so new demos need another home or a hidden preset opened by link.
- Measured Grand piano (`pianoType 'measured'`): tune `gainRelease` and master volume, then decide whether it replaces the piano in Ambient Piano and the other piano presets.
- The 2026-10-16 game / FTL / Synth Cinema preset batch and Plotting a Course: unheard, loudness never measured.

## 2. Not started

- **Range-aware composing** (user's decision 2026-11-02, for later): a narrow instrument is handled after the fact by `composition/registerShift.js`, one octave shift per phrase when its preset names a `range`, with single notes folding only as a last resort. The composer still writes in the role's usual range and does not know it will be moved, so a line can sit badly: cramped at the top of a range, or crossing another part after its shift. Later: hand the voice's range to the arranger as `ranges[role]`, so melody and voicing are written for it, and drop the shift where they are. Also still to do: role suggestions per instrument (crotales and bells as accents, not leads), and the Iowa voices themselves are not in the main app yet.
- **VST matching** ([VST_MATCHING.md](VST_MATCHING.md), `npm run vst:match`, 2026-11-03, uncommitted): headless Dexed / UNSTABLE / EightyEight / Deep Jupiter / OB-Xd renders compared with our FM7 / V 80 / Pluto 8 / Oberon Xa. FM7 is an exact twin of Dexed (32 factory voices measured; pitch EG, LFO depth, amp mod, release tails and fixed-frequency + fine voices differ). Amp envelope times calibrated for OB-Xd, UNSTABLE and EightyEight (tables in the doc; UNSTABLE's decay/release are linear in amplitude vs our exponential, so a shape fit is still open). OB-Xd spectral pass done (see doc): oscillator, 2-pole filter, pulse width, envelope amount and key-follow slope match; fixed in the engine: white noise, four-pole resonance taper (kernel rebuilt, uncommitted, not deployed); left: key-follow pivot (C2 vs middle C, deliberate), 4-pole stopband 2-6 dB, osc 2 detune not measured. Spectral and modulation passes done on EightyEight and UNSTABLE (both start a phantom note 60; the host kills it): filters, resonance, pulse width and envelopes match; Pluto 8 gained VCO 1 sine/noise. Engine now follows the plugin for Pluto 8 vibrato depth, tremolo, exponential cross-mod (to knob .3) and for V 80 resonance taper and PWM speed range (uncommitted, kernel rebuilt). Open: cross-mod above .3, sync, anything not yet measured (Oracle 5, Analog 106, Mercury D, VectorVS have no VST). Next: LFO/PWM, and the differing FM7 features.
- **Classic synth quality (user report 2026-10-29)**: only a handful of presets are good - V 80 (Plain, Electric Tines, Velvet Strings, Soft Bow), Analog 106 (Classic Pad, Wide Pulse Pad, Strange Machine, Rimshot, Alto Reed, Nylon, Bass 106), Mercury D (Singing Filter); those are listed in the main-page picker (library/classics.json). On the others (Oracle 5, Pluto 8, Oberon Xa, FM7, VectorVS...) almost every preset sounds bad - weird wobble, "off" - which points to an underlying engine bug, not patch tuning. Needs real reference recordings to compare against before fixing; start by rendering one preset per synth offline and looking for the shared fault (voice detune/drift, unison, LFO, filter tracking).
- Composition: A2 lead density arc, A3 rubato vs syncopation, B3 lead compass, B4 tonal-stability prior, C5 pad subset per attack, C6 low-spacing roughness, E3-E5 harmony and form, G2-G4 generated-progression follow-ups.
- **Rhythm and pad as separate layers** (`layers`: pad / rhythm / split / combined, `layerFlow` over time), [SPLIT_LAYERS_PLAN.md](SPLIT_LAYERS_PLAN.md): stages 1-4 and 6 built (engine, composition rules, UI, links, randomizer, sequencer lanes), layer voices for 110 presets, two demos; all uncommitted. Open: listening (the voice picks in `.tmp/layer-voices.md`, the rhythm part's high register, loudness of converted presets, the journey and sections flows), the plan's other showcase presets (5.3), quests for the layers. Stage 7 of the same plan, built after that: playable voicings (`voicing`: free / hand / guitar / auto; measured 2026-10-31: 22% of pad chords wider than an octave, 82% guitar-fingerable). Stage 8: tuning to the key / chords instead of equal temperament via the existing per-note `detune`.
- **Beyond the major scale** ([WORLD_MUSIC_PLAN.md](WORLD_MUSIC_PLAN.md), 2026-10-31): 95% of presets are in the seven major-scale modes; plan for drone and non-tertian textures, scale tuning, melodic idioms, ornaments, rhythm cycles, heterophony and form. Survey and staged goals only; shares hooks with the layers and voicing work.
- Swap short-pattern pads to Deep Pulse in 7 presets (Bounty Hunter Basement, Heroic Field Trip, Dark Mirror Dimension, Boss Fight Politely, Demo Opening out, Nightcall Voicemail, Getaway Driver). User said "fine for now".
- 365 "sound of the week" preset generator, also wired into randomize.
- Mobile low-CPU mode (share one reverb per impulse type, fewer layers, pre-render). Pixel 8 Pro is CPU-bound; see `DESIGN.md` section 5.
- Loudness audit of all presets (offline LUFS check).
- Link `/synths` from the main page.
- Pedal / sympathetic resonance for the piano.
- Single-room reverb mode on the shared bus; TB-303 as a bass source.
- Drum-style share field is full (16 of 16): a 17th style needs a new appended field.
