What is in Hand Percussion?

Low and muted conga, bongo, woodblock, claves, seed shaker, tambourine tap/shake, palm clap, cabasa and a seed sweep. Rounded modal drum bodies and quieter metallic textures make it a softer acoustic palette. Groove selection is independent: try the hand-percussion groove or bossa, then compare with the same notes on another kit.

What changes in the 808-inspired kit?

Longer pitched sine kicks, two tuned snare bodies under filtered noise, a three-burst clap, tuned rim/tom voices, six inharmonic square oscillators for hats and cymbals, and a two-oscillator cowbell. Brush swish becomes an open metallic hat; soft/bright hats choke it with a short fade. The voice changes the instruments and balance; groove, activity and variation remain separate rhythm controls. The cowbell is quieter in the 808 preset to sit under the main beat. The current-recipes model keeps brush swishes and the original timbres.
