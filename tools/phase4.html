Phase 4 · common sound comparison & audit

Hear the earlier sound behavior against the current expression layers on one fixed score. Current keeps the longer preset decay and release. Beat linking and short-note envelope fitting remain opt-in; optional versions make their tradeoff easy to hear. Continuous pitch motion is also opt-in: a slow, gentle contour carries through notes, arpeggios and release tails. The separate haunted chord-slide example bends one changing chord tone.

What is checked, and what needs listening?

The raw audit renders the selected voice at its role gain and real polyphony: three velocities, low/high register, short/held notes, chords, dense writing, forced voice stealing, no-note silence, cancellation of already booked notes, and resuming after cancellation. It measures floating-point samples before export processing, tail levels and sampled mix/limiter reduction. Each active case includes a 20-second tail window. Findings flag full-scale output, non-finite samples, excessive DC, unexpected silence, late tails or failed cancellation/resume; they are diagnostic, not a perceptual score.

The listening comparison uses the same pitches, beats, velocities and written lengths for all versions. Generated sound recipes may differ. It uses the normal WAV export, including the export's final fade and soft limiting; raw audit results are separate. Sections, ambient sound and drift are off for isolation. Use the held/chord passages and disable RMS matching to judge natural fullness too. Music remains the essential listening check.
