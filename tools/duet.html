Retained-motif duet comparison

This page tests one specific interaction strategy: retained-motif repeats between the lead and counter. Compare independent second-lead lines with a repeated idea in one voice while the other moves, followed by exchanged roles, contrary motion and a shared return. Other approaches, including parallel harmony, are outside this comparison.

Both versions use the same sounds, harmony and phrase rules. This harness enables a continuous second lead and sets repeat preference to zero so the interaction is audible. The app keeps its existing activity controls and parallel-harmony option. Sixteen bars show oblique and contrary motion; 32 also includes the inverse exchange. Repeated pitches adapt when a held clash or changing harmony needs it. Exact timing defaults on; ambience, sections and drift are off.
